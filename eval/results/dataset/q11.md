For the Sigma 14mm F1.8 DG HSM | Art on the Sigma MC-11 with a Sony α7S III: AF-S is supported, AF-C is not supported, and DMF is supported.

Why:
- Sigma’s MC-11 lens table lists this lens as: “14mm F1.8 DG HSM” — Sigma, p. 1.
- The same row says “AFS ○, AFC/AFA ×, DMF ○.” — Sigma, p. 1.
- That gives you single-shot AF (AF-S) working, continuous AF (AF-C) not working, and DMF working on the MC-11.

What to do:
- Use AF-S or DMF for stills.
- Don’t expect AF-C on this adapter/lens combination.
- For video, Sigma separately says: “Please use MF when shooting movies.” — Sigma, p. 1.

With the Sigma MC-11, the Sigma 35mm F1.4 DG HSM | Art is listed as AF-S supported, AF-C not supported, and DMF not supported for general shooting on the Sony α7 IV.

Why:
- Sigma MC-11 lens table row for “35mm F1.4 DG HSM” says: “AFS ○, AFC/AFA ×, DMF ×.” Sigma, page 1.
- There is also a video note from Sigma: “Please use MF when shooting movies.” Sigma, page 1. That is specifically for video, not stills.

What to do:
- For stills, expect AF-S to work, but do not expect AF-C or DMF through the MC-11 with this lens.
- If you need continuous AF or DMF, this dataset does not show support for that combo.

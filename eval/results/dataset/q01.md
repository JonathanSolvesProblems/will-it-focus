Through the Sigma MC-11 on a Sony a7 IV, the Sigma 35mm F1.4 DG HSM Art is supported for AF-S, not supported for AF-C, and DMF is not supported.

Why:
- Sigma’s MC-11 lens table row for the 35mm F1.4 DG HSM shows “○ × ×” for AFS / AFC-AFA / DMF, and the record maps that to:
  - single AF: supported
  - continuous AF: not supported
  - DMF: not supported
- There’s also an MC-11 compatibility note stating: “The camera's AF-C is not supported.” Sigma, p. 1.
- I didn’t find any focus caveat in the dataset for this exact body + lens + adapter combination.

What to do:
- Use AF-S only.
- Don’t expect AF-C or DMF through the MC-11 with this lens.
- If focus still misses, the dataset here does not provide a manufacturer caveat for this exact setup, so I can’t give a sourced reason from this database.

For the Sigma 30mm F1.4 DC HSM Art on a Sony a6100 through the MC-11: AF-S works, AF-C does not, and DMF does not.

Why:
- Sigma’s MC-11 lens table for the 30mm F1.4 DC HSM says: “30mm F1.4 DC HSM” — Sigma, p. 1. The table note says “AFS ○, AFC/AFA ×, DMF ×.” — Sigma, p. 1.
- A separate Sigma MC-11 note says “Please use MF when shooting movies.” — Sigma, p. 1. That is for video, not your photo AF modes.
- No focus caveat in the dataset applies to this exact body/lens/adapter combo beyond the teleconverter warning, which does not apply here unless you add a teleconverter.

What to do:
- Use AF-S for single-shot autofocus.
- Don’t expect AF-C or DMF to work with this setup.
- If you’re shooting video, Sigma’s guidance is to use manual focus.

On a Sony α9 II via Sigma MC-11: AF-S is supported, AF-C is not supported, and DMF is not supported.

Why:
- Sigma’s MC-11 lens table for the 120-300mm F2.8 DG OS HSM shows: “120-300mm F2.8 DG OS HSM ○ × ×” — Sigma, p.1.
- The MC-11 compatibility table also says: “The camera's AF-C is not supported.” — Sigma, p.1.

What to do:
- Use single-shot AF only.
- Don’t expect continuous AF or DMF with this lens/adapter pairing on the a9 II.

Yes — continuous autofocus works while recording video with the Canon EOS Rebel T5i and the EF-S 18-55mm IS STM kit lens.

Reason:
- Canon’s lens compatibility note says this lens is compatible with the EOS REBEL T5i/700D, and the record for video says “Quiet, smooth Movie Servo AF.”
- The compatibility record for this body/lens/video combination says continuous AF is supported.

What to watch for:
- Canon also warns that with some lenses, focusing noise can be recorded during Movie Servo AF. For this kit lens, Canon points to STM as the quieter choice.
- That caveat is about noise, not AF failure.

Manufacturer source quoted:
- “Function compatible with the following camera (as of June 2013): EOS REBEL T5i/700D, EOS REBEL SL1/100D, EOS REBEL T4i/650D, EOS M (when using with Mount Adapter EF-EOS M)” — Canon, p. 2
- “With certain lenses, the lens mechanical sound during focusing may be recorded.” — Canon, p. 196

Verdict: AF-S works, AF-C does not, and DMF is not supported through the Sigma MC-11 with the Sigma 35mm F1.4 DG HSM Art on a Sony α7 IV.

Why:
- Sigma’s MC-11 lens table for the 35mm F1.4 DG HSM shows AFS as supported, AFC/AFA as not supported, and DMF as not supported: “35mm F1.4 DG HSM ○ × ×” — Sigma, p. 1.
- The MC-11 compatibility table also says: “The camera's AF-C is not supported.” — Sigma, p. 1.
- No separate body-specific restriction for the α7 IV was needed here; the lens/adapter table already covers the combination.

What to do:
- Use AF-S for stills.
- Do not expect AF-C to work through the MC-11 with this lens.
- If you need video, Sigma says to use MF when shooting movies with the MC-11.
- If focus still misses, the dataset’s published caveat here is about video/manual focus, not a failure mode for AF-S stills.

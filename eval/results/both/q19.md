No — with the Sigma MC-11, a teleconverter in between means autofocus is not covered by the manufacturer sources in this dataset, because Sigma explicitly says the MC-11 “Cannot be used with teleconverter.” Sigma Global, MC-11 compatibility table, p. 1.

Without the teleconverter:
- For the Sigma 70-200mm F2.8 DG OS HSM | Sports on the MC-11, single AF is supported.
- Continuous AF is not supported.

What to do:
- Remove the teleconverter if you want the MC-11 AF behavior documented by Sigma.
- If you must keep the teleconverter, the dataset has no manufacturer source covering autofocus for that chain, so I can’t confirm AF.

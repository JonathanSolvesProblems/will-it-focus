Sigma α9 II + Sigma 120-300mm F2.8 DG OS HSM Sports via MC-11: AF-S supported, AF-C not supported, DMF not supported.

Why:
- The MC-11 lens table includes **120-300mm F2.8 DG OS HSM** with **AFS ○, AFC/AFA ×, DMF ×**. Sigma’s table note says that means AFS is supported, AFC/AFA is not supported, and DMF is not supported. Source: Sigma, MC-11 lens compatibility table, p. 1.
- The adapter record for the MC-11 SA-to-E says **“The camera's AF-C is not supported.”** Source: Sigma, MC-11 compatibility table, p. 1.

What to do:
- For stills, use **AF-S / One-Shot style focusing**.
- Do **not** expect continuous tracking AF-C through the MC-11 with this lens.
- DMF is also **not supported** on this lens in the MC-11 table.
- I did not find any separate focus-caveat record for this exact body/lens/adapter combination in the dataset.

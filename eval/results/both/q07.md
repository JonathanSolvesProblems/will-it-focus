Supported for AF-S and DMF, not supported for AF-C on the Sony α7 IV with the Sigma MC-11.

Why:
- The MC-11 lens compatibility table has a row for the Sigma 150-600mm F5-6.3 DG OS HSM Contemporary: “150-600mm F5-6.3 DG OS HSM ○ × ○” — Sigma, MC-11 lens compatibility table, page 1.
- That row is summarized in the dataset as AFS supported, AFC/AFA not supported, DMF supported.
- The MC-11 table also says “The camera's AF-C is not supported.” — Sigma, MC-11 compatibility table, page 1.

What to do:
- Use AF-S for still subjects and DMF if you want manual fine-tuning after focus acquisition.
- Do not expect AF-C tracking with this lens on the MC-11.
- If focus still misses, the manufacturer source here does not give a body-specific exception for the a7 IV; the published limitation is the adapter/lens combination itself.

Yes — continuous AF during video is supported for the Canon EOS 100D / Rebel SL1 with the Canon EF-S 18-55mm f/3.5-5.6 IS STM.

Why:
- The compatibility record for video says continuous AF is **supported** and notes: “Function compatible with the following camera (as of June 2013): EOS REBEL T5i/700D, EOS REBEL SL1/100D, EOS REBEL T4i/650D, EOS M (when using with Mount Adapter EF-EOS M)” Canon, p. 2.
- The knowledge base summary says the STM version enables quiet, smooth Movie Servo AF during video on the SL1/100D, and that Movie Servo AF is available in movie mode.

What to do:
- Use Movie Servo AF enabled in video mode.
- If focus still misses, the manufacturer notes that fast-moving subjects approaching or receding, or subjects very close to the camera, are difficult to focus.
- Also, the lens manual says to press the shutter button halfway to wake the STM lens if it is off or asleep.

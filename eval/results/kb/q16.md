**Verdict — Live View photos:** With a Canon T5i/700D and EF 50mm f/1.8 II, Face+Tracking and FlexiZone AF are **limited**: Canon’s Live View guidance specifically recommends **Quick mode** for this lens because the contrast-detect Live View methods may focus slowly or fail.

## Reasons

- The `live_view_autofocus` source summary says the T5i/700D offers Face+Tracking, FlexiZone-Multi, FlexiZone-Single, and Quick mode in Live View still shooting.
- For the **EF 50mm f/1.8 II**, that same source file says Canon specifically recommends **Quick mode**; the contrast-detect methods — Face+Tracking, FlexiZone-Multi, and FlexiZone-Single — may be slow or may fail with this lens.
- The same source explains why misses can still happen in Live View: contrast-detect AF struggles with low contrast, low light, repetitive patterns, small or edge-of-frame subjects, mixed near/far subjects in one AF point, moving subjects, and subjects that start far out of focus. Live View contrast-detect AF is also slower than viewfinder AF, and Live View does not use the normal AF-assist beam.

## What to do

- If you want the most reliable Live View AF with this lens, use **Quick mode** instead of Face+Tracking or FlexiZone. It uses the camera’s viewfinder phase-detect AF sensor, but the Live View image is interrupted briefly while the mirror flips down.
- If you must use Face+Tracking or FlexiZone, use strong contrast, good light, and place the AF area over a clear edge or detail.
- For critical focus, use **FlexiZone-Single with magnification** and be ready to switch to manual focus if AF hunts or fails.

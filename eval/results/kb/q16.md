Verdict: on a Canon T5i in Live View, the EF 50mm f/1.8 II can autofocus with Face+Tracking or FlexiZone, but Canon says those contrast-detect modes may focus slowly or fail; Quick mode is recommended for this lens.

Reasons:
- The T5i’s Live View AF methods include u (Face)+Tracking, FlexiZone-Multi, and FlexiZone-Single; Quick mode uses the phase-detect AF sensor instead of contrast-detect.
- Canon specifically lists the EF50mm f/1.8 II as one of the lenses for which Quick mode is recommended because the contrast-detect methods “may focus slowly or fail.”
- Face+Tracking and FlexiZone are still available with this lens; the source does not say they are unsupported, only that they may be unreliable.
- I’m not seeing a separate manufacturer record here that gives a more exact success rate for Face+Tracking versus FlexiZone on the EF 50mm f/1.8 II; the published guidance is the same caution for all contrast-detect Live View methods.

What to do:
- If you want the most dependable focus in Live View with this lens, use Quick mode.
- If you use Face+Tracking or FlexiZone, expect slower acquisition and possible hunting/failure, especially in low-contrast or otherwise difficult scenes.
- If focus accuracy matters more than Live View convenience, consider magnified view for confirmation.

**Verdict — still photos on Sony α6000 with Sigma MC-11: AF-S is supported; AF-C is not supported; DMF is not supported for this lens.**

Reasons:

- The KB entry `adapter_compatibility/sigma_mc11` summarizes Sigma’s MC-11 compatibility tables and lists the Sony **α6000** as a compatible E-mount body.
- In the same MC-11 lens table, the **Sigma 17-70mm F2.8-4 DC MACRO OS HSM Contemporary** is listed as:
  - **AFS: supported**
  - **AFC/AFA: not supported**
  - **DMF: not supported**
- For video, the same KB entry says Sigma instructs users to use **manual focus** with the MC-11.

What to do:

- Use **AF-S** for stills.
- Do **not** expect reliable/available **AF-C tracking** on this combo.
- Do **not** expect **DMF** with this particular lens on MC-11.
- If focus still misses in AF-S, switch to MF and confirm focus manually; the MC-11 source summary also notes compatibility is confirmed but not guaranteed for all functions/performance.

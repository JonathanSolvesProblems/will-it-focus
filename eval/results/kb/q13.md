Yes—continuous autofocus works during video recording on the Canon EOS Rebel T5i with the EF-S 18-55mm IS STM kit lens.

Why:
- The T5i’s Movie Servo AF is available in movie mode and, when enabled, “Camera continuously focuses on moving subjects during recording.” The STM kit lens is specifically listed as minimizing focusing noise in recording.
- The knowledge base summary for the T5i says the EF-S 18-55mm f/3.5-5.6 IS STM is compatible with Movie Servo AF on this body.

What to do:
- Turn Movie Servo AF on in movie mode.
- If you want less focus noise, the STM lens is the right kit lens for that.
- If focus still misses, the KB notes that fast-moving subjects, subjects moving toward/away from the camera, and very close subjects are harder to keep in focus during Movie Servo AF.

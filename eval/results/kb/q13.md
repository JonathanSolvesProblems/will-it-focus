Yes — continuous autofocus works during video recording with the Canon EOS Rebel T5i and the EF-S 18-55mm IS STM kit lens.

Reason: the T5i’s Movie Servo AF is available in movie mode and the EF-S 18-55mm f/3.5-5.6 IS STM is specifically listed as compatible. The knowledge base summary says Movie Servo AF “continuously focuses on moving subjects during recording,” and the lens entry says the STM version enables “quiet, smooth Movie Servo AF” during video.

What to do:
- Turn Movie Servo AF on in movie mode.
- Expect it to be contrast-detect/live-view style AF, so it can be slower than viewfinder AF.
- If the subject is fast-moving, close to the camera, or the scene is low-contrast, focus may hunt or miss; the summary notes those are difficult conditions.

Relevant manufacturer wording from the dataset:
- “Enable: Camera continuously focuses on moving subjects during recording.” Canon manual, p. 2.
- “The EF-S18-55mm f/3.5-5.6 IS STM and EF-S18-135mm f/3.5-5.6 IS STM lenses minimize focusing noise in the recording” Canon manual, p. 2.

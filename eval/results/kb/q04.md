For the Sigma 24-70mm F2.8 DG OS HSM Art on MC-11 with Sony a7 III: AF-S works, AF-C does not, and DMF works.

Why:
- The MC-11 table lists the **24-70mm F2.8 DG OS HSM** as **AFS: supported** and **AFC/AFA: unsupported**.
- The same table shows **DMF: supported** for that lens.
- The Sony a7 III is in Sigma’s confirmed compatible body list for the MC-11.

What to do:
- Use **AF-S / One Shot** for autofocus.
- Do **not** expect **AF-C** tracking to work with this adapter/lens combination.
- **DMF is available** on this lens with the MC-11.
- For movie shooting, Sigma’s MC-11 guidance says to use **MF**.

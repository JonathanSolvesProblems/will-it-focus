**Verdict:** On the Sony a7 III with the Sigma MC-11 and Sigma 24-70mm F2.8 DG OS HSM Art, **AF-S works, AF-C does not work, and DMF works for stills; for video, Sigma says to use MF.**

## By shooting mode

### Still photos
- **AF-S:** Supported. The MC-11 compatibility summary lists the α7III as a compatible Sony E-mount body and lists the 24-70mm F2.8 DG OS HSM Art as **AFS: ○**.
- **AF-C:** Not supported. The MC-11 summary says AF-C/AFA are unsupported for all listed lenses.
- **DMF:** Supported for this lens, but the summary notes DMF availability may require changing settings via the **Sigma USB Dock**.

Source summarized: `adapter_compatibility/sigma_mc11`, citing `sigma-mc-11-camera-compatibility.pdf` and `sigma-mc-11-lens-compatibility.pdf`.

### Video
- Sigma’s MC-11 summary says to use **manual focus** when shooting movies. So don’t expect AF-S, AF-C, or DMF behavior in video from this source.

## If focus still misses
Use **AF-S for stills**, avoid **AF-C**, and configure **DMF with the USB Dock** if needed. For movies, switch to **MF**.

Verdict: AF-S is supported, AF-C is not supported, and DMF is supported for the Sigma 85mm F1.4 DG HSM Art on the Sony a7C with the MC-11.

Why:
- The MC-11 compatibility table lists the Sony a7C among compatible bodies.
- In the Art lens table, the 85mm F1.4 DG HSM shows AFS as supported, AFC/AFA as unsupported, and DMF as supported.
- The same document also says AF-C is not supported on any body, and that you should use MF when shooting movies.

What to do about it:
- Use AF-S for stills.
- Do not expect AF-C to work with this adapter-lens combination.
- DMF is available on this lens, but Sigma notes that DMF availability depends on lens settings via the USB Dock.
- For video, Sigma’s published guidance is to use manual focus.

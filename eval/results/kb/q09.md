Verdict: AF-S is supported, AF-C is not supported, and DMF is supported for the Sigma 85mm F1.4 DG HSM Art on the MC-11 with a Sony a7C.

Why:
- The MC-11 compatibility table lists the Sony α7C as a compatible body.
- In the Art lens table, Sigma 85mm F1.4 DG HSM has AFS marked supported and DMF marked supported.
- The same MC-11 source says AFC/AFA are unsupported for all lenses and “AF-C is not supported on any body.”

What to do about it:
- Use AF-S for autofocus stills.
- Do not expect AF-C tracking through the MC-11; if you need continuous tracking, this combination is not supported by the manufacturer source.
- DMF is available on this lens, but Sigma notes DMF availability may require USB Dock settings; for video, Sigma says to use MF.

Yes — continuous AF during video is supported for the Canon EOS 100D / Rebel SL1 with the EF-S 18-55mm f/3.5-5.6 IS STM.

Why:
- The lens summary says the EF-S 18-55mm f/3.5-5.6 IS STM enables “Quiet, smooth Movie Servo AF (continuous AF during video)” and that Movie Servo AF is compatible with EOS REBEL SL1/100D.
- The body summary says Movie Servo AF provides continuous AF in movie mode, and that this works with Canon STM lenses such as the EF-S 18-55mm f/3.5-5.6 IS STM.

What to do:
- Enable Movie Servo AF in the camera’s movie settings.
- If you want the smoothest and quietest focusing during recording, keep the lens in AF and let Movie Servo AF do the work.
- If focus still misses, the knowledge base says fast-moving subjects approaching/receding, close subjects, low contrast, low light, or subjects initially far out of focus can be difficult for Live View/movie AF.

**Verdict for stills on Sony a6400 + Sigma MC-11 + Sigma 50mm F1.4 DG HSM Art: AF-S works, AF-C does not work, and DMF is available but conditional.**

## Reasons

- The Sony **α6400** is listed in the Sigma MC-11 compatible Sony E-mount body list, summarized in `adapter_compatibility/sigma_mc11`.
- In Sigma’s MC-11 per-lens table summarized there, the **50mm F1.4 DG HSM** Art lens is marked:
  - **AFS: supported**
  - **AFC/AFA: not supported**
  - **DMF: supported**
- The same source summary says **AF-C is not supported on any body** with the MC-11.
- For **video**, the Sigma MC-11 source summary says to use **manual focus**, so don’t rely on AF-S/AF-C/DMF for movie shooting with this adapter setup.

## What to do

- Use **AF-S** for stills.
- Do **not** use **AF-C** for tracking; misses there are expected because Sigma does not support AF-C with MC-11.
- For **DMF**, enable/configure it as required; the Sigma MC-11 summary notes DMF may require changing settings via the **Sigma USB Dock**.
- For video, switch to **MF**.

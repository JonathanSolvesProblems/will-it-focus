**Verdict — Video continuous AF: No, not supported.** With the Sigma 50mm F1.4 DG HSM Art on a Sony a6400 via the Sigma MC-11, the sources say to use manual focus for movies; it will not keep focus continuously while recording video.

- For the MC-11, the knowledge base summary says Sigma lists the **Sony α6400** as a checked compatible E-mount body.
- For the **Sigma 50mm F1.4 DG HSM Art**, the MC-11 lens table lists **AFS supported**, **AFC/AFA not supported**, and **DMF supported**.

**What to do**

- For video with this adapted lens: plan to **focus manually**.
- If you need continuous autofocus while recording, use a lens/adapter combination with manufacturer-confirmed video AF-C support—do not assume it from physical fit.
- DMF availability for this lens does not change the movie-shooting restriction.

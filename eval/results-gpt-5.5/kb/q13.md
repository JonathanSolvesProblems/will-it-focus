**Verdict — Video recording:** Yes. On the Canon EOS Rebel T5i / EOS 700D with the EF-S 18-55mm f/3.5-5.6 IS STM, continuous autofocus during video is supported via Movie Servo AF.

**Reasons:**  
- The T5i/700D has Movie Servo AF in movie mode, and when enabled it continuously focuses on moving subjects during recording. Source summary: `live_view_autofocus`, citing `canon-eos-rebel-t5i-700d-instruction-manual.pdf`.
- The EF-S 18-55mm IS STM is specifically described as an STM lens suited to quiet, smooth Movie Servo AF on compatible bodies including the Rebel T5i/700D. Source summary: `lens_autofocus`, citing `canon-ef-s-18-55-is-stm-instructions.pdf`.
- The T5i launch/specification summary also lists the EF-S 18-55mm IS STM as compatible with the camera’s Hybrid CMOS AF and Movie Servo AF. Source summary: `camera_body/specifications`.

**What to do:**  
- Put the camera in movie mode.
- Set **Movie Servo AF: Enable**.
- Use one of the movie AF methods: Face+Tracking, FlexiZone-Multi, or FlexiZone-Single.
- If focus still misses, likely causes include fast subjects moving toward/away from the camera, very close subjects, low contrast, low light, small subjects, or mixed near/far subjects in the AF area. Movie Servo AF can also pause briefly during zooming or magnified view.

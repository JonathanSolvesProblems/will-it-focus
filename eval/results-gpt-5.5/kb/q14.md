**Video continuous AF verdict: supported** — the Canon EOS 100D / Rebel SL1 and EF-S 18-55mm f/3.5-5.6 IS STM are covered as a Movie Servo AF combination.

**Reasons**
- For video, Canon’s Movie Servo AF is the relevant continuous-AF mode.
- The knowledge-base summary for `lens_autofocus` says the EF-S 18-55mm f/3.5-5.6 IS STM supports Movie Servo AF on specific bodies including the EOS Rebel SL1 / 100D, and that STM provides smooth, quiet continuous focusing during video.
- The `live_view_autofocus` summary notes that Movie Servo AF continuously focuses on moving subjects during recording, and that the EF-S 18-55mm f/3.5-5.6 IS STM is one of the lenses that minimizes focusing noise in the recording.

**If focus still misses**
- Movie Servo AF can struggle with fast subjects moving toward/away from the camera, very close subjects, low contrast, low light, repetitive patterns, very small subjects, or mixed near/far subjects in the AF area (`live_view_autofocus`).
- Remedy: keep Movie Servo AF enabled for continuous video AF, use a suitable AF method such as Face+Tracking or FlexiZone, improve light/contrast, avoid very close or fast approach/recede movement when possible, or switch to manual focus for critical shots.

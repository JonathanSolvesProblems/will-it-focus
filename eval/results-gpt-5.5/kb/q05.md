Verdict: **On the Sony a7R IV with the Sigma MC-11, the Sigma 24-105mm F4 DG OS HSM Art is covered for still-photo AF-S, but AF-C and DMF are not supported.**

**Still photos**
- **AF-S:** supported. The knowledge base entry `adapter_compatibility/sigma_mc11` summarizes Sigma’s MC-11 lens table as listing the **24-105mm F4 DG OS HSM** with **AFS: ○**.
- **AF-C:** not supported. The same entry summarizes Sigma’s global MC-11 restriction that **AF-C is not supported on any body**, and the per-lens row for this lens shows **AFC/AFA: ×**.
- **DMF:** not supported for this lens. The per-lens row for **24-105mm F4 DG OS HSM** shows **DMF: ×**.
- **Body coverage:** the a7R IV / α7RIV is included in the MC-11 compatible Sony E-mount body list in `adapter_compatibility/sigma_mc11`.

**Video**
- The same source summary says to **use MF when shooting movies** with the MC-11, so do not rely on AF-S/AF-C/DMF for video.

**What to do**
- Use **AF-S for still subjects**.
- Do **not** expect AF-C tracking to work through the MC-11.
- Do **not** expect DMF on this particular 24-105mm Art through the MC-11.
- For video, switch to **manual focus**.

Verdict: **On the Sony α9 II with Sigma MC-11, this 120-300 Sports is covered for stills with AF-S supported and AF-C not supported; DMF is not covered by a dataset verdict.**

## Reasons

### Stills / general AF use
- The dataset has a Sigma MC-11 compatibility record for **Sigma 120-300mm F2.8 DG OS HSM | Sports** covering both **MC-11 Canon EF–E** and **MC-11 Sigma SA–E**.
- That record says:
  - **AF-S / single AF:** supported
  - **AF-C / continuous AF:** not supported
  - **DMF:** not stated in the dataset record

Manufacturer source quote for the lens row: “120-300mm F2.8 DG OS HSM” — Sigma, page 1.

The MC-11 adapter source also says it “allows you to use your SIGMA SA-mount and Sigma EOS mount interchangeable lenses with the Sony E-mount camera body.” — Sigma, page not stated.

The α9 II appears in the dataset’s tested-body list for both MC-11 variants.

### Video
Sigma’s MC-11 video record in the dataset says to use manual focus for movies: “Please use MF when shooting movies.” — Sigma, page 1.

### DMF note
The dataset has no `dmf` verdict for this combination, so I can’t mark DMF supported or not supported from the dataset verdicts. The knowledge-base summary for `adapter_compatibility/sigma_mc11`, citing `sigma-mc-11-lens-compatibility.pdf`, says DMF availability varies by lens and that the 120-300mm F2.8 DG OS HSM Sports row is marked unavailable, but that is a KB summary rather than a dataset compatibility-record verdict.

## What to do

- Use **AF-S** for stills.
- Do **not** expect **AF-C / tracking AF** through the MC-11.
- For movies, use **MF**.
- Avoid teleconverters with the MC-11: the dataset caveat says “Cannot be used with teleconverter.” — Sigma, page 1.

**Verdict: On an MC-11 with a Sony α6600, the Sigma 18-35mm F1.8 DC HSM | Art supports AF-S for stills, but not AF-C or DMF; for video, Sigma says to use MF.**

## Reasons

### Stills / general MC-11 use
- The dataset’s Sigma MC-11 lens compatibility record covers the **Sigma 18-35mm F1.8 DC HSM | Art** on both **Sigma MC-11 Canon EF-E** and **Sigma MC-11 Sigma SA-E** variants.
- It marks:
  - **singleAf / AF-S: supported**
  - **continuousAf / AF-C: notSupported**
  - Its note says the Sigma table row is **AFS ○, AFC/AFA ×, DMF ×**, so **DMF is not supported** for this lens.
- Manufacturer source quote: “18-35mm F1.8 DC HSM” — Sigma, p. 1.

The knowledge-base summary in `adapter_compatibility/sigma_mc11` matches this: it summarizes Sigma’s MC-11 lens table as AF-S supported for the 18-35mm F1.8 DC HSM, AF-C/AFA unsupported, and DMF unavailable for this lens. It cites `sigma-mc-11-lens-compatibility.pdf`.

### Sony α6600 body coverage
- The dataset’s MC-11 adapter record lists **Sony α6600** among the tested/compatible Sony E-mount bodies for the MC-11.
- Manufacturer source quote for the adapter: “allows you to use your SIGMA SA-mount and Sigma EOS mount interchangeable lenses with the Sony E-mount camera body.” — Sigma, page not stated.

The knowledge-base summary in `adapter_compatibility/sigma_mc11` also lists **α6600** in Sigma’s compatible camera-body list, citing `sigma-mc-11-camera-compatibility.pdf`.

### Video
- The dataset has a separate MC-11 video record for this lens group.
- For video, it marks continuous AF as not supported and says single-shot AF is **unknown / not stated**.
- Manufacturer source quote: “Please use MF when shooting movies.” — Sigma, p. 1.

## What to do about it

- For still photography: use **AF-S**.
- Do **not** expect **AF-C tracking** with this lens on the MC-11.
- Do **not** expect **DMF** with the 18-35mm F1.8 DC HSM on the MC-11.
- For video: switch to **manual focus**.
- If focus still misses, this dataset does not give a lens/body-specific missed-focus caveat for the α6600 + Sigma 18-35mm + MC-11 combination. It does include one MC-11 caveat: do not use a teleconverter; Sigma says, “Cannot be used with teleconverter.” — Sigma, p. 1.

### Video
- The dataset has a separate video record for MC-11 listed lenses, including this 35mm, and its manufacturer source says: “Please use MF when shooting movies.” — Sigma, p. 1.
- So for movies, do not rely on AF; use manual focus.

### If focus still misses
- The only relevant caveat found in the dataset is about teleconverters: “Cannot be used with teleconverter.” — Sigma, p. 1. If you have a teleconverter in the chain, remove it.
